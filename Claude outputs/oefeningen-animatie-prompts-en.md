# Exercise animation prompts — Blijf Sterk (English)

One prompt per exercise, ready to paste into the AI animation/video field of your tool (e.g. Canva Magic Media, Animaker).

**Tips:**
- Pick a 3–4 second loop where possible (start and end pose match up).
- Vertical/portrait (9:16) works best for the app; square (1:1) also works.
- Got a result that looks too old or frail? Just generate again — the same prompt doesn't give the same result every time, so you can pick the best one.
- Each prompt deliberately describes a neutral, fictional adult — not a real person.

---

## 1. Marching in place (Warm-up)
An attractive, good-looking, vital and athletic adult in their late fifties / early sixties marches gently in place in a bright, tidy living room. Well-groomed appearance, radiant smile, healthy hair, great shape, the look of a professional fitness model — comparable to an active amateur athlete, NOT old, frail, or elderly-looking. Both knees rise gently in turn, arms swinging relaxed. Calm, even pace, no rush. Camera at eye level, straight-on, so the whole movement is easy to follow. Warm, natural lighting. Sporty, fresh-colored clothing (no sportswear brand logos). Calm, positive atmosphere.

## 2. Chair squat (Legs & balance)
The same attractive, vital and athletic adult (same age, same good-looking, energetic look — not old-looking) stands in front of a sturdy chair in a bright living room, feet hip-width apart. They bend their knees and lower slowly and in a controlled way, as if sitting down, lightly touch the chair, and rise back up calmly. Back straight, knees aligned with the feet. Slow, clear movement, looping. Camera straight-on at eye level.

## 3. Side leg raise with resistance band (Legs & balance)
The same person stands upright, holding a chair with one hand for support, with a resistance band around both ankles. The other leg lifts gently straight out to the side and lowers back down calmly, in a slow loop. Calm pace, small, controlled movement, resistance band clearly visible around the ankles. Bright, tidy living room, camera straight-on.

## 4. Calf raises (Legs & balance)
The person stands upright behind a chair, holding the backrest, rises gently onto their toes, holds it briefly, and lowers back down slowly. Smooth, slow up-and-down movement, looping. Clear view of feet and calves. Bright living room, camera at eye level, straight-on or slightly from the side.

## 5. Hip bridge / glute bridge (Core)
The person lies on their back on a fitness mat, knees bent, feet flat on the floor, arms relaxed at their sides. The hips lift gently up to a straight line from knees to shoulders, hold briefly, and lower back down slowly. Side view (profile), so the up-and-down hip movement is clearly visible. Calm, slow loop. Bright, tidy room with a mat on the floor.

## 6. Bird dog (Core)
The person is on hands and knees on a fitness mat, back straight, looking down at the floor. One arm extends gently forward while the opposite leg extends back, holds briefly, then returns calmly to the starting position. Slow, controlled movement, back stays straight. Side view (profile) on a bright, tidy floor.

## 7. Knee plank (Core)
The person holds a calm, still position on forearms and knees on a fitness mat, back in a straight line from head to knees, core engaged. No large movement — subtle, calm breathing visible in the body, as a short loop that holds the position. Side view (profile), calm, focused atmosphere, bright room.

## 8. Resistance band row (Back & shoulders)
The person sits upright, a resistance band anchored to something sturdy in front of them (e.g. a door handle) or under their feet, one end of the band in each hand, arms extended forward. The elbows pull back gently, as if squeezing the shoulder blades together, then the arms move slowly forward again. Calm loop, band clearly visible. Camera from the side or slightly angled, bright living room.

## 9. Band pull-apart — chest and shoulders (Back & shoulders)
The person stands upright, holding a resistance band with both hands in front of their chest. The arms move gently apart sideways until the band is taut, then move slowly back to the center. Slow, controlled loop, camera straight-on at eye level, bright living room.

## 10. Resistance band bicep curl (Arms)
The person stands with the resistance band under both feet, one end in each hand, elbows close to the body. The forearms curl gently up toward the shoulders and lower back down slowly. Calm, even loop, camera straight-on, bright living room.

## 11. Water bottle overhead press (Arms)
The person sits or stands upright, holding a filled water bottle in each hand at shoulder height. The bottles are pressed gently upward until the arms are (nearly) straight overhead, then lower back down slowly to shoulder height. Calm, controlled loop, camera straight-on at eye level, bright living room, water bottles clearly visible in both hands.

## 12. Water bottle side raise (Arms)
The person stands or sits upright, holding a filled water bottle in each hand, arms relaxed at their sides. The arms lift gently out to the sides to about shoulder height, then lower back down slowly. Slow, even loop, camera straight-on, bright living room, water bottles clearly visible.
